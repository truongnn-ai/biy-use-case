## Use Case 1



### SmartDBS. Deliverable-Based Services (DBS) Engagement Platform.



### The challenge: Everything is manual.

- **Email-Centric Process:** Requests for proposals, vendor submissions, clarifications, and communications are all managed through email, making information difficult to track and consolidate.
- **Manual Monitoring and Follow-Ups:** Proposal deadlines, vendor reminders, and follow-up communications depend on manual tracking, increasing the risk of delays and missed actions.
- **Labor-Intensive SOW Review:** Statement of Work (SOW) reviews are conducted manually, while Technical Evaluation Criteria (TEC) must be developed separately despite being closely linked to the requirements defined in the SOW.
- **Manual Evaluation and Reporting:** Vendor participation, proposal submissions, evaluation scores, rankings, and sourcing records are manually maintained in spreadsheets, limiting real-time visibility and creating additional administrative burden.



### Key capabilities

**AI-Assisted Requirement Development**
Review and improve SOW, identify gaps, and automatically generate suggested TEC to improve quality and consistency.

**End-to-end Vendor Engagement Workspace**
Centralize vendor RFPs, clarifications, proposal submissions, and communications in a single platform, eliminating fragmented email exchanges.

**Transparent Evaluation, Tracking and Reporting**
Maintain complete audit trail of vendor participation, submissions, scores, and outcomes. Centralized records provide greater visibility, easier tracking, and more transparent decision-making.

### Expected impact

- Operational efficiency: Reduce manual effort and administrative workload through automation and centralized workflows.
- Accelerated Process: Shorten sourcing cycle times with streamlined processes and AI-assisted reviews
- Enhanced transparency: Improve visibility, tracking, and governance through a platform.
- Strengthen Vendor Engagement – Foster better collaboration, participation, and responsiveness from vendor partners.



### SmartDBS

AI-powered engagement platform that reduces manual effort, streamlines vendor communication, and enables faster, smarter, and more transparent engagement process.

## Use Case 2



### Empower@BrandName Builders — On-Demand Digital Talent Marketplace



### The challenge: digital ideas move slowly when expertise is hard to find

**Pain point**
Departments have innovative ideas but may lack immediate access to specialized skills

**Opportunity**
Existing IDIQ resources may already be screened, provisioned, and ready to support delivery.

**Solution**
Enable specialists/talents discoverable through AI matching, portfolios, and availability visibility.

### Empower@BrandName Builders capabilities

- Dynamic profiles with CVs, certifications, skills, portfolios, and previous engagements.
- Smart search by Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Copilot Studio, Power Pages, Dataverse, SharePoint, AI, automation, and analytics.
- AI Talent Copilot to recommend specialists and project approaches from a plain-language business challenge.
- Skills intelligence dashboard to show availability, skills demand, utilization trends, and capability gaps.



### Benefits by audience

**For departments**
Faster access to expertise and shorter time from idea to working solution

**For ITD**
Better utilization of IDIQ resources and improved visibility of contractor capabilities.

**For BrandName**
Increased adoption, faster innovation, and greater return from existing contracts.

## Use Case 3



### VendorConnect AI — Smart Marketplace for Technology Innovation



### The challenge: supplier knowledge is fragmented

- Vendor information is dispersed across procurement records, business units, market events, and personal networks.
- Teams may spend weeks conducting market research or repeatedly engage the same suppliers.
- Promising or emerging vendors can be difficult to surface at the right time.
- There is no single searchable experience for BrandName staff to describe a need and discover relevant technology partners.



### VendorConnect AI: a living, searchable supplier ecosystem

**Vendor self-service portal**
Vendors maintain profiles, certifications, case studies, capability statements, contacts, and service offerings.

**AI-powered matching**
Staff describe a need in plain language and receive vendor recommendations with fit explanations.

**Comparison dashboard**
Compare capabilities, services, certifications, references, and shortlists for procurement planning.

### Expected outcomes

- Single source of truth for technology vendor capabilities.
- Faster project initiation and procurement planning.
- Broader supplier participation and stronger market intelligence.
- Improved transparency and knowledge sharing across BrandName.
- Scalable model that can expand beyond IT to other service categories.

Find the Right Partner. Accelerate Innovation. Deliver Impact.
