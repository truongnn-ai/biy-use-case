# Use Case 3 — Vendor ConnectAI

**Smart Marketplace for Technology Innovation**

*AI-assisted vendor sourcing, cross-validation, onboarding & offboarding*

## The challenge: supplier knowledge is fragmented

- Vendor information is dispersed across procurement records, business units, market events, and personal networks.
- Teams may spend weeks conducting market research or repeatedly engage the same suppliers.
- Vendor Lifecycle is manual, slow, and inconsistent across teams.
- There is no single searchable experience for BrandName staff to describe a need and discover relevant technology partners.



### Problem Statement

- Vendor information is fragmented across procurement records, business units, market engagement activities, and personal networks.
- Teams may spend considerable time conducting market research, searching for qualified suppliers, or repeatedly engaging the same vendors while potentially overlooking innovative solutions available in the market.
- No centralized, searchable platform where technology vendors can showcase their capabilities and where BrandName staff can easily discover vendors based on specific business and technology needs.



### Proposed Solution

**AI-enabled Vendor Service Catalog: a centralized self-service platform for vendor discovery, capability matching, and market intelligence.**

The platform would allow three categories of vendors to maintain their own profiles and service offerings:

- Potential Vendors (Business opportunity seminars, showcases)
- Registered Vendors (Registered in SIM)
- Existing Vendors (Active contracts)
- Support Vendor Lifecycle Governance (Onboarding & Offboarding)



## Your Hackathon Challenge

## Vendor Lifecycle Overview

**Phases (in order):**

1. **Pre-engagement & Contracting** — Vendor Registration
2. **Onboarding Kick-off** — Onboarding Checklist, Vendor Classification, Access Provisioning
3. **Orientation & Governance** — Vendor Governance, Onboarding Kit
4. **Implementation and Monitoring** — Score Card Review, Performance Evaluation Report (PER), Vendor Monitoring (i.e. SLA/KPI), Major Incident Handling
5. **Offboarding** — Offboarding Checklist
6. **Access Termination & Data Handling** — Access Termination, Data Destruction Certificate

**Responsible parties:** VMO, Contract Owner, Vendor, Risk Team, ITOD-CS MyAccess

> **Note:** VMO will provide the checklist to the vendor. The vendor must complete the checklist prior to the release of final payment.



### Vendor Onboarding Checklist


| Particulars                  | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Focal          |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| Contract Signed & Archived   | Vendor Manager (VM) to ensure that all signed contracts are properly saved in the VMO SharePoint.                                                                                                                                                                                                | VM             |
| Contract Delivery Log        | VM to complete the CDL before the onboarding orientation if applicable. Ensure inclusion of: Schedule 10 – Security Requirements; Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).                                                                                                                                | VM             |
| Background Checks            | For Managed Service Providers (MSPs), the vendor must provide an attestation confirming that all assigned resources have undergone background verification.                                                                                                                                      | Vendor         |
| Onboarding Orientation (Kit) | Conduct orientation session to: introduce Contract Owner, Vendor Focal, and VM; establish governance cadence and reporting schedule; define escalation procedures; discuss SLA & KPI framework; review cybersecurity guidelines. Attendees: VM, Contract Owner, Vendor, ITOD-CS Representatives. | VM / Risk team |
| IT Service Continuity Plan   | Business Continuity Plan & Disaster Recovery Plan must be submitted # days (depends on what is indicated on the contract) after the vendor orientation.                                                                                                                                          | Vendor         |
| Vendor Classification        | For new vendors, VCF process must be completed (can be done or parallel to onboarding).                                                                                                                                                                                                          | VM & User      |
| Access Provisioning          | User to initiate access application.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | User           |




### Vendor Offboarding Checklist


| Particulars                            | Description                                                                                                                                     | Focal          |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Performance Evaluation Report          | User to provide final PER.                                                                                                                      | Contract Owner |
| Notice of Non-renewal                  | CSPC to send out notice of non-renewal or termination.                                                                                          | CSPC Buyer     |
| Contract Review                        | VM to review contract termination clauses and initiate exit plan as per contract or based on the submitted exit plan during onboarding.         | Vendor Manager |
| Vendor Exit Score Card (if applicable) | User to finalize vendor exit scorecard.                                                                                                         | Contract Owner |
| Exit Plan (if applicable)              | Vendor and user to align disengagement timelines and responsibilities.                                                                          | Vendor         |
| Knowledge Transfer (if applicable)     | Vendor to: submit technical documentation, credentials, and source code; close outstanding deliverables; provide final project/service reports. | Vendor         |
| Certificate of Data Destruction        | This is to certify data return or destruction. User to validate compliance with privacy, security, and retention obligations.                   | Vendor         |
| Access Revocation                      | Coordinate account deactivation with ITOD-CS MyAccess. Audit and log the deactivation process.                                                  | MyAccess       |
| Billing                                | Vendor to submit final billing. BrandName to settle final billing upon completion of offboarding checklist.                                     | Finance        |
